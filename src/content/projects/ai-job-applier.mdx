## the problem

job hunting is a numbers game with diminishing returns. tailoring resumes, filtering listings, and submitting applications is hours of repetitive work per day. meanwhile, most applications are screened by automated ATS systems anyway.

if the screening is automated, the applying should be too.

## what it does

the agent deeply understands your professional profile — multiple resume versions, experience details, preferences — and runs the full application pipeline autonomously:

1. **email parsing** — monitors Gmail for LinkedIn daily job alert emails
2. **filtering** — evaluates each listing against role, location, company size, and compensation preferences
3. **fit evaluation** — assesses the job description against your actual experience and interests
4. **resume tailoring** — selects the right base resume and adjusts emphasis to match the job description
5. **submission** — applies automatically via browser automation
6. **tracking** — logs application status and surfaces follow-up opportunities

nothing gets submitted that you haven't pre-approved at the profile level. the agent executes your preferences, not its own.

## patterns used

- **email parsing** — structured extraction from Gmail alerts
- **document generation** — resume tailoring with Claude
- **preference matching** — profile-driven filtering
- **browser automation** — form-based application submission
- **multi-resume management** — versioned resume library with context-aware selection
