## the problem

evaluating stocks for halal investing is a multi-step, manual process: compliance screening, fundamental analysis, earnings review, analyst signal checks, and technical charting. each stock takes significant time — and the watchlist keeps growing.

most tools either stop at screening (compliance only) or stop at fundamentals (ignoring Sharia constraints entirely). none of them do the full process end to end.

## what it does

the agent takes an unfiltered watchlist collected from various sources and runs every stock through a structured analysis pipeline:

1. **Sharia compliance screening** — filters based on business activity, debt ratios, and income sources
2. **Fundamental analysis** — P/E ratios, recent earnings, revenue trends, financial health
3. **Analyst signals** — aggregated sentiment and analyst ratings
4. **Technical analysis** — support/resistance bands, trend identification, entry and exit levels
5. **Summary output** — a structured record per qualified stock with rationale and suggested levels

the watchlist is persistent. the agent re-evaluates stocks as conditions change and flags when a position's thesis breaks.

## patterns used

- **multi-step research agent** — sequential, dependent analysis stages per stock
- **financial data retrieval** — pulling from market data APIs
- **technical chart analysis** — programmatic TA using Python (pandas-ta, mplfinance)
- **persistent watchlist management** — stateful tracking across runs
